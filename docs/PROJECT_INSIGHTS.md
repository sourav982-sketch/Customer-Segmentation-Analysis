# Customer Segmentation Project – Insights

## Project Objective
The objective of this project is to segment customers based on their demographic information and purchasing behavior.

## Segmentation Method
K-Means clustering was used to group customers based on:
- Annual Income
- Purchase Frequency
- Average Order Value
- Total Spend
- Website Visits

The numerical features were standardized before applying K-Means clustering.

## Key Analysis
The dashboard helps analyze:
- Customer segments
- Purchase frequency
- Total customer spending
- Income and spending relationship
- Customer preferences
- Gender and city distribution

## Business Insights
- High-value customers can be identified based on their spending and purchase behavior.
- Customers with frequent purchases represent an important group for retention.
- Customers with high website visits but lower purchases can be targeted with personalized offers.
- Customer segmentation can help businesses design targeted marketing strategies.
